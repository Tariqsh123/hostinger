Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from our clients about our services and processes.

What is prototype development?
Prototype development involves creating an interactive model of your app or product to visualize features, design, and functionality before full development begins.
Why should I build a prototype first?
A prototype helps validate ideas, gather user feedback, and refine functionality early—saving time and costs during full-scale development.
How long does it take to develop a prototype?
Most prototypes are completed in 2–6 weeks, depending on complexity, number of screens, and required interactivity.
Do you provide both design and interactive features?
Yes. We create high-fidelity UI/UX designs with clickable, interactive elements to showcase real user flows and functionality.
How can I start a prototype development project?
Share your concept and requirements via our contact form or email. We’ll schedule a consultation to discuss goals, features, and timeline.
