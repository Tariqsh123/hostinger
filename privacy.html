Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how our publishing company (“we,” “our,” “us”) collects, processes, and safeguards personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. It applies to all clients, contributors, visitors, and anyone interacting with our publishing or creative services.

Who We Are

We are an independent publishing service provider offering editing, design, formatting, marketing, and distribution support. As a data controller, we determine how and why your information is processed and ensure it is handled responsibly and securely.

For privacy-related enquiries or data protection matters, please contact our Data Protection Officer (DPO).

Personal Data We Collect

Clients and Authors

  • Full name, contact address, and communication details
  • Submitted manuscripts, illustrations, and project files
  • Billing and payment records
  • Publishing contracts or royalty agreements
  • Marketing or communication preferences

Website Visitors and Enquiries

  • Information provided via contact forms
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser type
  • Cookies and analytical data for performance insights

Business Partners

  • Company details and contact information
  • Registration numbers and relevant documentation
  • Correspondence and partnership records

We do not intentionally collect data from individuals under 18 years of age.

How We Use Your Data

Fulfilling Contracts

To manage submissions, production, and distribution processes for our clients and partners.

Legal Obligations

To maintain compliance with accounting, tax, and data protection laws.

Legitimate Interests

To enhance service quality, prevent fraud, and ensure smooth operational delivery.

Consent-Based Activities

To send newsletters, updates, or marketing communications that users have opted into.

  • Administering contracts and publishing schedules
  • Providing ISBN or distribution support
  • Processing payments and invoices
  • Responding to client and customer requests
  • Sending relevant updates or promotional content (where permitted)

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies to improve user experience, monitor performance, and personalise content. You can control or disable cookies in your browser settings, though essential cookies remain required for certain features. Analytical data may be collected by trusted providers under GDPR-compliant agreements.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Personal data may be shared only when necessary, including with:

  • Printing or production partners
  • Freelancers such as editors and designers under confidentiality terms
  • Secure payment processors
  • Legal or regulatory authorities if required by law
  • Technology and hosting providers ensuring system reliability

Personal data is never sold or traded with third parties.

International Data Transfers

Where service providers operate outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or legally approved transfer mechanisms.

Data Retention

  • Publishing and manuscript records: up to 7 years after project completion
  • Financial data: 6 years, as required by law
  • Marketing data: retained until consent is withdrawn
  • General enquiries: retained up to 12 months

After these periods, all data is securely deleted or anonymised.

Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Update or amend inaccurate details
  • Deletion: Request erasure where legally allowed
  • Restriction: Limit how your data is used
  • Portability: Request data in a digital format
  • Objection: Withdraw from marketing or other processing
  • Automation: We do not engage in automated or profiling decisions

All requests will be handled in accordance with data protection laws and responded to within 30 days.

Data Security

We apply strict security measures including encryption, secure servers, SSL certificates, and restricted access controls. Regular technical reviews and audits are conducted to ensure ongoing protection. While we prioritise data safety, no system is entirely immune from risk; users are encouraged to maintain password confidentiality.

Marketing Communications

Subscribers may receive occasional updates or service information. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by contacting our team or using the unsubscribe option provided in communications.

Payment Handling

We do not store full payment card details. Transactions are processed securely by third-party providers that comply with international security standards such as PCI-DSS.

External Links

Our platform may contain links to other websites or resources. These are provided for convenience, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Users are encouraged to review the privacy statements of any external sites they visit.

Complaints and Contact

Individuals with concerns about data handling may contact our data protection team for assistance. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant supervisory authority for data protection in your country.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically to reflect legal or operational changes. Updated versions will be published on our platform, and continued use signifies acceptance of the latest terms.
